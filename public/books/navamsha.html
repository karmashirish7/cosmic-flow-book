IntroductionWhat these notes are

The Rashi chart (D1) shows the life you were handed. The Navamsha (D9) cuts every sign into nine slices and shows what grows out of it: what you give, what you become, and what your marriage and your second half of life turn into. That is why the D9 is called the chart of bhagya, or fortune.

This series reads the D9 one sign at a time. For each Navamsha sign it asks four questions: what the sign itself means, what each planet does inside it, what happens when it falls in each house of the D9, and how the planet's original Rashi sign colours the result. These notes keep that order.

Read this first

Everything here is the teacher's framework, recorded as taught, not tested fact. The examples marked “worked example” are made up by these notes to show the method step by step; the short “from class” cases are ones the teacher discussed. Libra Navamsha was never taught in the series (the teacher left it for last and for his book), so its chapter is only a placeholder. Use these readings to ask better questions about a chart, and confirm anything important from three or four directions before you say it out loud.

Before you startKey words you will meet

Rashi chart (D1)राशि The ordinary birth chart. In these notes: the event, the trigger, what you received.
Navamsha (D9)नवांश “Ninth part.” Each 30° sign is split into nine slices of 3°20′. In these notes: the fruit, the result, what you give and become.
Pada / amshaपाद One slice of 3°20′. There are 12 × 9 = 108 of them around the zodiac, the same as the 108 nakshatra quarters.
Navamsha sign The sign a planet lands in once you work out its slice. “Mars in Aries Navamsha” means Mars's slice maps to Aries.
Source sign The planet's sign in the D1. The series reads it as “Rashi → Navamsha”: blend the D1 sign's nature into the Navamsha sign.
Navamsha lagna The sign the ascendant's slice falls in. It is house 1 of the D9; houses are counted from it.
Vargottamaवर्गोत्तम A planet in the same sign in D1 and D9 (e.g. Taurus → Taurus Navamsha). The sign's effect comes through at full strength.
Exalted / debilitated A planet's best and worst signs. In a Navamsha, a debilitated planet's significations go missing; an exalted one can be enjoyed.
Marriage lens Most house readings in the series look at the D9 as the marriage chart: what the house shows about spouse, in-laws and married life.
Chara karakas Jaimini's movable significators by degree (Atmakaraka = highest degree, Darakaraka = lowest). The teacher uses the 8-karaka scheme with Rahu, taken from the D1.
Shadbala / Bhava Bala Strength scores. The teacher's rough lines: Shadbala about 420+ (of 450) is strong; Bhava Bala about 25+ is good.
Dashavatara deity Each planet is linked to one of Vishnu's ten forms (Sun–Rama, Mars–Narasimha, Rahu–Varaha …). Remedies in the series lean on these.
Part I · FoundationsHow the D9 is built, and the method used for every sign.

Principle 1 & 2D1 is the seed, D9 is the fruit

The single most repeated idea in the series: the D1 shows that something happens; the D9 shows what comes of it and whom it touches. When a planet's dasha runs, it gives the results of its Navamsha sign and its D9 house.

D1 · RashiThe event, the condition, the trigger.
  • What you received at birth
  • Shows strongly from childhood
  • “You got married”
→
D9 · NavamshaThe fruit, the result, the after-effect.
  • What you give and become
  • Sprouts slowly, mostly after work or marriage
  • “What the marriage turned into, and who it affected”
Example · how the two charts split a story

Suppose the D1 shows a job change in a Mars period. The D1 tells you the change happens. The D9 tells you whether that change sets you up (Mars in Capricorn Navamsha: big deals, machines, land), keeps you fighting (Mars in Aries Navamsha: authority won through struggle), or drains you emotionally (Mars in Cancer Navamsha, where it is debilitated: emotion turns into fights).

Principle 5Working out a planet's Navamsha by hand

Every sign is 30°. Cut it into nine equal slices of 3°20′ and you get nine padas. The only question is where the counting starts, and that depends on the element of the D1 sign:

D1 sign groupElementCounting starts fromSo the nine slices run
Aries, Leo, Sagittarius (1-5-9)fireAriesAries → Sagittarius
Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn (2-6-10)earthCapricornCapricorn → Virgo
Gemini, Libra, Aquarius (3-7-11)airLibraLibra → Gemini

A memory trick: each element starts from the movable sign of its own element. Fire starts at Aries, earth at Capricorn, air at Libra, water at Cancer.

Worked example · Venus at 25° Libra
  1. Libra is an air sign, so counting starts from Libra.
  2. 25° ÷ 3°20′ = 7.5, so Venus is in the 8th slice (slices 1–7 cover 0°–23°20′; the 8th covers 23°20′–26°40′).
  3. Count 8 signs starting with Libra: Libra 1, Scorpio 2, Sagittarius 3, Capricorn 4, Aquarius 5, Pisces 6, Aries 7, Taurus 8.
  4. Venus is in Taurus Navamsha, its own sign. The series calls this exact slice out: Libra 23°20′–26°40′ → Taurus, “marriage brings money, fights or division; a best-deal mindset.”

Exclusion rulesWho can reach which Navamsha

Because each element runs through only nine signs, three Navamsha signs are always out of reach. The series uses this all the time: if a Navamsha can never be formed from a sign, that sign's “nightmare” is that Navamsha.

Principle 3How to read a planet: the four steps

The teacher insists on this order. Starting from the planet first, he says, is the commonest reason predictions go wrong. The sign outweighs the planet.

Navamsha signWhat is this sign's nature? (its core traits)
PlanetWhat does this planet do inside that sign?
D9 houseWhich area of life (and which relative) carries it?
Source signBlend in the D1 sign it came from.

Then cross-check with the dasha that is running, the planet's strength, aspects and the chara karakas, and only say it if it holds from several directions.

Worked example · Saturn at 27° Cancer

Cancer is a water sign, so counting starts at Cancer. 27° is the 9th slice (26°40′–30°), and nine signs from Cancer lands on Pisces. Say the Navamsha lagna is Virgo, so Pisces is the 7th house of the D9.

  1. Sign: Pisces Navamsha is the moksha sign. Everyone leaves so you learn to stand on your own; it rewards charity and surrender.
  2. Planet: Saturn here makes a sannyasi-type, a Shiva bhakta, night shifts, results-oriented charity, a full-stack worker.
  3. House: Pisces in the 7th: the relationship feels like deity worship, one-sided, with reciprocation delayed.
  4. Source: Cancer → Pisces: must leave home, may settle abroad; “Saturn neutralises attachment (the coconut).”

Put together: a partner who is served with devotion but answers late, a working life of long or night hours, and home left behind. Now check the running dasha and Saturn's strength before saying any of it.

Adjacent-sign logicThe journey through the signs

The teacher often explains a Navamsha by the sign before and after it. Read the twelve as one story:

  • Aries starts → Taurus sustains → Gemini reacts and expresses. Aries begins things; Taurus decides whether they survive (capital, support); Gemini is what comes after money: art, talk, play.
  • Leo rules → Virgo maintains. The throne, then the daily upkeep and SOPs that keep it.
  • Scorpio wounds → Sagittarius tests and protects → Capricorn binds. Misconduct in Sagittarius ends in bonded labour in Capricorn.
  • Capricorn (karma) → Aquarius (results, desires fulfilled) → Pisces (release).
  • Opposites: Cancer vs Capricorn (mood vs compulsory routine); Aquarius vs Leo (no territory, no regular income vs the throne).
  • Leo ↔ Sagittarius: Sagittarius → Leo Navamsha = rise and fame. Leo → Sagittarius Navamsha = risk of falling from position. Pisces is the 8th from Leo: the fall from status.

TechniquesDiagnostic sutras worth remembering

D9 superimposition (from Cancer)

Place another person's planets on your D9 and see which falls on your D9 Moon's house. Rahu, Venus or Jupiter there = love. Mars = irritation. Saturn = drain and stalled work. Sun = they give orders.

Money fights and dowry (from Taurus)

The D9 2nd sign = what your family is abused over in fights. The D9 8th sign = what you pick on in your spouse. For physical violence check the 3rd and 9th, Mars, and the D4.

Venus is the seed (from Taurus)

Where Venus sits in the D9 is the seed of your money tree; water it. Venus houses are “grey”: money from them is never fully clean, unlike Jupiter's.

Position timing (from Leo)

Leo Navamsha activating = getting position; Sagittarius = falling from it. In Lagnamsha (Navamsha-lagna) rashi dasha, Leo–Sagittarius sub-periods test your position. Whatever drama you hide tends to surface in an Aquarius–Sagittarius period.

When Mercury readings fail (from Virgo)

Find what suppresses Mercury: Jupiter = family training, Mars = peer or group pressure, a strong Moon = no rules.

Handle with care

The series also gives “litmus tests” for cheating (Jupiter in Capricorn Navamsha plus other factors, confirmed in D45/D81) and markers for violence. These are never single-factor readings. The teacher himself says to confirm from several charts and dashas. Don't announce them from one placement.

RevisionOne-page summary

Method

D1 = event, D9 = fruit. Read sign → planet → house → source sign. Validate from 3–4 directions. The sign outweighs the planet.

Calculation

3°20′ slices. Fire from Aries, earth from Capricorn, air from Libra, water from Cancer. Vargottama: 1st slice of movable, 5th of fixed, 9th of dual signs.
